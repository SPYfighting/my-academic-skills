## Research anchor

This project uses **paper-anchor**. Before designing research, running analyses, reviewing evidence, or handing off scientific work, load the skill and read the root `PAPER.md`. Link formal work to the relevant claim or evidence ID. Authorized exploration can proceed before an ID exists, with findings recorded for later review. The researcher confirms the thesis, claim changes, and final verdicts. Maintain evidence and propose changes with supporting records. Keep execution details and artifacts at their original locations and link to them from `PAPER.md`.
